調香師Perfumers

同一個調香師的作品常有相似的“筆觸”。愛上一支之後，順著調香師找，命中率通常比順著品牌找更高。A perfumer's works often share a recognisable “handwriting”. Once you love one scent, following the perfumer is usually a better bet than following the brand.

香調入門Scent basics

香調就是香水的“大類口味”。先認得這十四種，逛專櫃時就能用它們描述你要什麼。Scent families are the broad “flavours” of perfume. Learn these fourteen and you can describe what you want at any counter.

前調、中調、後調

  • 前調：噴上後 5–15 分鐘，通常是柑橘、草本，最快散掉。
  • 中調：之後 1–3 小時，香水的“性格”，多是花香、香料。
  • 後調：留在皮膚上最久，木質、琥珀、麝香、香草。
  • 專櫃第一口聞到的只是前調，別只憑它決定。

第一次試香的做法

  1. 先用試香紙篩，一次最多 3–4 支，鼻子會疲乏。
  2. 留下最喜歡的 1–2 支，噴在手腕上。
  3. 離開櫃檯，30 分鐘後和 3 小時後各聞一次。
  4. 還喜歡的話，先買小樣或分裝，用一週再決定正裝。

Top, heart and base notes

  • Top: the first 5–15 minutes, usually citrus or herbs; fades fastest.
  • Heart: the next 1–3 hours, the perfume's character — often florals and spices.
  • Base: lingers longest on skin — woods, amber, musk, vanilla.
  • The first sniff at a counter is only the top notes; don't decide on that alone.

How to sample for the first time

  1. Screen with paper strips first — 3–4 at most, or your nose tires.
  2. Keep your 1–2 favourites and spray them on your wrists.
  3. Walk away; smell again after 30 minutes and after 3 hours.
  4. Still love it? Buy a sample or decant and wear it for a week before a full bottle.
